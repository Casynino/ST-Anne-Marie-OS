# Design system

Decisions made with the `ui-ux-pro-max` design skill (searches for style, typography, colour, chart and UX rules) and checked in the browser on desktop, tablet and phone widths. Three looks, one per audience.

## 1. Public website: bright school (structure studied from istafrica.co.tz, look in the school's own colours)
- **History:** three looks were tried and rejected (template-like green mesh; pale "paper and ink"; dark cinematic that copied Nino's Vegas hotel site too closely). Nino then asked for the *structure and features* of a real school website (International School of Tanganyika) in the school's own identity. His other sites are inspiration for polish and motion only; never copy them or another school's text, photos or branding.
- **Look:** white pages, deep-green navigation and bands, the school's yellow as accent (offset block behind photographs, bottom border on cards, main buttons), Montserrat headings + Inter text, real photographs, rounded-corner cards with a yellow foot (`.card-y`). Tokens re-declared on `.site-root` (light), `.site-soft` (tinted band), `.site-deep` (deep green band, white text); accent colour token `text-accent` is green on light, yellow on deep. Never hard-code colours on public pages.
- **Structure (taken from the reference):** identity row (emblem, name, motto; Calendar, Quick links ▾, search, language, Parent portal, Apply) + sticky deep-green nav bar with drop-down menus built from the school's own content; announcement bar; photo hero; Inquire / Visit / Apply tabs on the right edge (wide screens) and a bottom bar on phones; Welcome block with photo and yellow offset; Levels cards; **News & Events** boxes (latest news with thumbnails, upcoming events with dated circles, RSS and calendar-alert icons, term progress, "view full calendar"); photo tiles; admissions steps; family portal preview; Instagram strip; contact; footer.
- **Features added with it:** `/calendar` (month grid with terms and public events, categories, list for phones, `/calendar.ics` subscription feed), `/news/rss.xml`, `/search` (published news, public events, page text), `/apply/visit` (visit request, saved as an enquiry with topic Visit), `/student-life` (uniform, transport, meals, health, clubs; each block appears only when the school has written it), About sections for facilities and safeguarding, a grouped menu, home "Happening at school" (term week and days left from the real term dates).
- **Identity:** the logo is the school's emblem cut from its own Instagram poster (`public/site/logo.png`, also the browser icon); posters for admissions are in `public/site/notices/` and used as news pictures. Source photos are ~640 px (Instagram previews): ask the school for originals. Nothing is invented; no stock photos.
- **Motion (framer-motion + CSS, all off under `prefers-reduced-motion`):** hero slideshow with slow drift and a pause button, word-by-word headline, scroll reveals, counters, live school time, a horizontal photo rail pinned while scrolling (swipe strip on phones), the scroll-tilt portal device.

## 2. Staff area: data-dense dashboard, dark by default
- **Style (refreshed 2026-10-22, Nino: "the font and shapes feel off"):** data-dense dashboard. **Inter** with tabular numbers (`.app-root`; Fira Sans was dropped), calmer corners (`--radius-2xl` 12px and `--radius-xl` 10px inside `.app-root`, public site unchanged), table headings in plain sentence case (an unlayered `.app-root thead th` rule), underline tabs instead of boxed chips (`AdmissionsNav`, `FinanceNav`, `CountTabs`), lighter button weights. **Sidebar** (`components/app-shell/sidebar.tsx`): a floating rounded panel like Nino's hotel dashboard: logo, school name and "School management", the home items, then each group with an icon heading and its pages under a thin guide line, the current page as a soft block with a yellow mark on the line, Sign out and Collapse at the foot under a dashed line. Dark theme `.theme-night` (cookie `sam_theme`, toggle in the top bar and portal header) is a set of semantic tokens (`bg-surface`, `bg-canvas`, `text-ink`, `text-muted`, `border-line`, `text-link`, `text-heading`, `bg-tint`, `tone-*`), so a screen never hard-codes a colour.
- **Home screens are role-driven:** admin / head / accountant / registrar see the permission-gated school overview (`dashboard.ts`: hero with one plain sentence built from real numbers, jump chips, KPI cards, "needs attention" list with tabs, charts and tables); a teacher (class or subject, no school-wide student access) sees their own day (`teacher-home.ts`, `dashboard/teacher.tsx`): lessons today, registers to take, homework to mark, mark sheets, planning, classes, recent behaviour notes.
- **Charts:** dependency-free SVG, each with a legend, native hover values, and a hidden table with the same numbers. Colour is never the only signal.
- **Monitoring** (sign-ins, errors, jobs, backups) is not on the Overview; it lives at Settings → System health and Backups.
- **Width:** shell content up to 112rem.

## 3. Family portal (parents and students)
Same dark design system. Child page = hero (child's name, one sentence from real figures), four KPI cards (attendance, fees owed, latest report, homework), quick tiles, today's lessons, homework table, attendance donut with recent days, report cards table with PDF. A student sees the same page, greeted by name. Everything a family can read goes through `portal.ts`.

## Pre-delivery checklist (used for every screen)
Contrast ≥ 4.5:1 in light and dark; focus rings kept; touch targets ≥ 44 px; no horizontal scroll at 375 px; no emoji as icons and no sparkle icons; labelled icon buttons; reduced-motion respected; empty, loading and error states present; numbers formatted once (tabular); nothing invented (no made-up statistics, photos or text).
