# Teacher guide / Mwongozo wa mwalimu

*Maelezo ya Kiswahili yako sehemu ya 2.*

## 1. English
You only see the classes, streams and subjects the school has assigned to you. If something you expect is missing, ask the office to check your assignment under **Academics**.

**Attendance** — open **Attendance → Take**, mark every student (Present, Absent, Late, Excused), then **finalise**. A register is finalised only when every student is marked. A finalised register changes only through a recorded correction. No signal? Download the class list first on the *Offline attendance* page; entries sync later and show *synced* only after the school's server confirms.
**Marks** — **Marks** lists your mark sheets. Enter scores (an empty box means *not entered*, it is never a zero; use *Absent*/*Excused* where it applies), **save** often, then **submit**. The head teacher approves and locks. Late submissions after the deadline are flagged.
**Timetable** — **Timetable** shows yours by default. Only the office builds it.
**Planning** — write a **scheme of work** (weeks, topics, objectives, activities) and **lesson plans** for classes you teach; **send for review**. If returned, read the reviewer's note, change it, send again. After the lesson add a *reflection*.
**Assignments (homework)** — *New assignment*: choose class/stream and subject, write instructions, due date and time (school time) and, if wanted, a maximum score. It stays a **draft** until you **publish**; then students and parents see it. Attach worksheets (PDF or photo, up to 4 MB, 5 files). When work arrives, open the assignment and each student: read the answer, enter a **score** and **feedback**, and either **Save mark** or **Return for revision** (the student hands in again). Marks are private until you tick *Share…* or press *Share … with families*. For work on paper use **Record paper work**. **Close** the assignment when the time is over; a closed one takes no more work (you can reopen).
**Portfolio** — add a piece of work or a moment for a student; it is visible to the family at once. Pieces students add wait in your list: **Publish** or **Remove** (with a reason).
**Behaviour** — **Record behaviour**: Positive, Concern or Incident. Write facts. It cannot be edited afterwards; a wrong record is **voided** with a reason. A record reaches parents only if you choose *Share with family*. Students see only shared *positive* notes.
**Messages** — **Communication → Messages**: you can write to parents of students you teach (when the parent has an account) and answer their messages. Be respectful: the school office can read every conversation.

Good habits: finalise attendance the same morning; never share your password; sign out on shared computers.

## 2. Kiswahili
Unaona tu madarasa, mikondo na masomo uliyopangiwa na shule. Kama kuna kitu unachotarajia hakipo, muulize ofisi akague mgawo wako kwenye **Taaluma**.

**Mahudhurio** — fungua **Mahudhurio → Chukua**, weka hali ya kila mwanafunzi (Yupo, Hayupo, Amechelewa, Ana udhuru), kisha **thibitisha**. Daftari linathibitishwa wakati kila mwanafunzi ameandikwa tu. Daftari lililothibitishwa hubadilishwa kwa marekebisho yaliyorekodiwa tu. Hakuna mtandao? Pakua orodha ya darasa kwanza kwenye ukurasa wa *Mahudhurio bila mtandao*; yanatumwa baadaye na huonyesha *yametumwa* baada ya seva ya shule kuthibitisha tu.
**Alama** — **Alama** inaorodhesha mafaili yako ya alama. Weka alama (kisanduku tupu kinamaanisha *haijawekwa*, si sifuri; tumia *Hakuwepo*/*Udhuru* inapohusika), **hifadhi** mara kwa mara, kisha **wasilisha**. Mwalimu mkuu huidhinisha na kufunga. Kuwasilisha baada ya muda huwekewa alama.
**Ratiba** — **Ratiba** huonyesha yako kwanza. Ofisi pekee huijenga.
**Mipango ya kufundisha** — andika **maandalio ya mwaka** (wiki, mada, malengo, shughuli) na **maandalio ya somo** kwa madarasa unayofundisha; **tuma kupitiwa**. Ikirudishwa, soma ujumbe wa mhakiki, rekebisha, tuma tena. Baada ya somo ongeza *tafakari*.
**Kazi za nyumbani** — *Kazi mpya*: chagua darasa/mkondo na somo, andika maelekezo, tarehe na saa ya mwisho (saa za shule) na, ukitaka, alama za juu. Inabaki **rasimu** hadi **uchapishe**; ndipo wanafunzi na wazazi wanaiona. Ambatisha karatasi (PDF au picha, hadi MB 4, faili 5). Kazi ikifika, fungua kazi na kila mwanafunzi: soma jibu, weka **alama** na **maoni**, kisha **Hifadhi alama** au **Rudisha kwa marekebisho** (mwanafunzi anawasilisha tena). Alama ni siri hadi ubonyeze *Mshirikishe…* au *Washirikishe familia…*. Kwa kazi ya karatasi tumia **Weka kazi ya karatasi**. **Funga** kazi muda ukiisha; iliyofungwa haipokei kazi tena (unaweza kuifungua).
**Jalada la kazi** — weka kazi au tukio la mwanafunzi; familia inaliona mara moja. Vitu wanavyoweka wanafunzi vinasubiri kwenye orodha yako: **Chapisha** au **Ondoa** (kwa sababu).
**Tabia** — **Rekodi tabia**: Chanya, Wasiwasi au Tukio. Andika ukweli. Haiwezi kuhaririwa baadaye; kumbukumbu isiyo sahihi **hufutwa** kwa sababu. Wazazi wanaipata ukichagua *Shirikisha familia* tu. Wanafunzi huona tu taarifa *chanya* zilizoshirikishwa.
**Ujumbe** — **Mawasiliano → Ujumbe**: unaweza kuandikia wazazi wa wanafunzi unaowafundisha (wenye akaunti) na kujibu ujumbe wao. Kuwa na heshima: ofisi ya shule inaweza kusoma mazungumzo yote.

Tabia njema: thibitisha mahudhurio asubuhi ileile; usishiriki nenosiri; toka kwenye kompyuta za pamoja.
