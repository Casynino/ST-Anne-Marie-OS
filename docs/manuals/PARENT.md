# Parent and student guide / Mwongozo wa mzazi na mwanafunzi

*Maelezo ya Kiswahili yako sehemu ya 2.*

## 1. English
### Getting your account
The school office creates your portal account and gives you a **temporary password** in person. Sign in at the school website → **Parent Portal** → **Sign in**, using your e-mail or phone number. You must choose a new password the first time. Keep it private; the school will never ask for it by phone or message. Forgot it? Ask the office to reset it.
Parents see only their own children. Students (if the school gave them an account) see only themselves.

### What you can see
- **Home**: your child, with colour-coded shortcuts: **Homework**, **Timetable**, **Portfolio**, **Behaviour notes**, **Fees**; and attendance, fees owed, latest report.
- **Homework**: what is set, when it is due, the teacher's feedback and the mark — but marks appear only after the teacher shares them. **Parents cannot hand work in**: students do, with the answer written in a box and/or photos or PDFs (up to 5 files, 4 MB each). If the teacher returns it, read the note and hand in again. Late work is marked *late*.
- **Timetable**: the class lessons week by week.
- **Portfolio**: pieces of work the teacher has approved. Students can add their own; a teacher looks at it first.
- **Behaviour notes**: only what the school chose to share with you. A student account sees positive notes only.
- **Report cards**: only published ones; you can open or download the PDF.
- **Fees**: invoices, payments, receipts and how to pay. A receipt exists only after the school records the payment.
- **Messages**: write to your child's class teacher or the school office. Messages cannot be edited or deleted; the school office may read them.
- **Inbox / Notices / Calendar**: school notices, reminders, results ready, events. In **Settings** choose which notices come by SMS or in the app.

### If something looks wrong
Do not use another family's link or details. Tell the school office; they can see exactly what happened, when and by whom.

## 2. Kiswahili
### Kupata akaunti yako
Ofisi ya shule hutengeneza akaunti yako ya mlango wa familia na kukupa **nenosiri la muda** ana kwa ana. Ingia kupitia tovuti ya shule → **Mlango wa wazazi** → **Ingia**, kwa barua pepe au namba ya simu. Lazima uchague nenosiri jipya mara ya kwanza. Liweke siri; shule haitakuuliza kamwe kwa simu au ujumbe. Umesahau? Waombe ofisi wakuwekee upya.
Wazazi huona watoto wao tu. Wanafunzi (shule ikiwapa akaunti) huona wao wenyewe tu.

### Unachoweza kuona
- **Nyumbani**: mtoto wako, na njia za mkato zenye rangi: **Kazi za nyumbani**, **Ratiba**, **Jalada la kazi**, **Taarifa za tabia**, **Ada**; pamoja na mahudhurio, ada inayodaiwa, ripoti ya mwisho.
- **Kazi za nyumbani**: zilizotolewa, muda wa mwisho, maoni ya mwalimu na alama — lakini alama huonekana baada ya mwalimu kuzishirikisha tu. **Wazazi hawawasilishi kazi**: wanafunzi ndio huwasilisha, kwa jibu lililoandikwa kwenye kisanduku na/au picha au PDF (hadi faili 5, MB 4 kila moja). Mwalimu akirudisha, soma ujumbe na uwasilishe tena. Kazi iliyochelewa huwekwa alama *imechelewa*.
- **Ratiba**: vipindi vya darasa wiki kwa wiki.
- **Jalada la kazi**: kazi zilizoidhinishwa na mwalimu. Wanafunzi wanaweza kuweka zao; mwalimu huziangalia kwanza.
- **Taarifa za tabia**: ile tu ambayo shule iliamua kukushirikisha. Akaunti ya mwanafunzi huona taarifa chanya tu.
- **Ripoti za maendeleo**: zilizochapishwa tu; unaweza kuzifungua au kupakua PDF.
- **Ada**: ankara, malipo, risiti na jinsi ya kulipa. Risiti ipo baada ya shule kurekodi malipo tu.
- **Ujumbe**: mwandikie mwalimu wa darasa wa mtoto wako au ofisi ya shule. Ujumbe hauwezi kuhaririwa wala kufutwa; ofisi ya shule inaweza kuusoma.
- **Kikasha / Taarifa / Kalenda**: taarifa za shule, vikumbusho, matokeo tayari, matukio. Kwenye **Mipangilio** chagua taarifa zipi zije kwa SMS au kwenye programu.

### Kama kitu kinaonekana si sahihi
Usitumie kiungo au taarifa za familia nyingine. Mwambie ofisi ya shule; wanaweza kuona kilichotokea, lini na nani.
