# Accountant guide / Mwongozo wa mhasibu

*Maelezo ya Kiswahili yako sehemu ya 2.*

## 1. English
All money is in TZS. Amounts are exact (no rounding errors). **Finance** has: Overview, Invoices, Payments, Refunds, Reconciliation, Fees, Expenses, Reports.

**Set up once per year** — *Fees → Fee items* (Tuition, Transport…), then a **fee structure** per class, level or for everyone (the most specific one wins; a line can be every term or one term only). Grant **scholarships/discounts** per student and year (percent or amount, with a reason).
**Each term** — *Invoices → Generate term invoices*: choose the term and due date, read the **preview** (who will be billed, who has no structure, zero-total invoices), then generate. Running it again never bills anyone twice. Use *Adjustments* (extra lines) and *Instalments* when needed; *Void* an invoice with a reason.
**Receiving money** — *Payments → Record payment*: find the student, enter the amount, method (cash, bank, mobile money) and the bank slip or transaction number. A numbered **receipt** (PDF, English or Kiswahili) is made. The same slip or reference cannot be entered twice. Money not aimed at an invoice pays the oldest unpaid invoice first; extra money stays as **credit**.
**Mistakes** — never edit: **Reverse** the payment with a reason (not possible after its money was refunded). **Refunds** only return credit: request → a *different* person approves → mark paid.
**Mobile-money/bank notifications** — until a provider is connected nothing arrives automatically. When one is, anything unclear waits in **Reconciliation** for you to match or reject; nothing counts as paid until it is matched.
**Expenses** — record with category; they cannot be deleted.
**Reports** — collections by day and method, by class, arrears (with ageing), expenses. Each can be exported to CSV, Excel or PDF; exports are logged.
**Reminders** — *Communication → Fee reminders*: choose who owes (overdue or all), preview the exact parents and the amount per child, send or schedule. SMS shows *skipped* until an SMS provider is connected.

Remember: *paid*, *partly paid*, *unpaid* and *zero charge* are different states. An invoice existing, or being zero, never means a payment was received.

## 2. Kiswahili
Pesa yote ni TZS. Kiasi ni sahihi kabisa. **Fedha** ina: Muhtasari, Ankara, Malipo, Marejesho, Upatanisho, Ada, Matumizi, Ripoti.

**Mipangilio mara moja kwa mwaka** — *Ada → Vipengele vya ada* (Ada ya masomo, Usafiri…), kisha **muundo wa ada** kwa kila darasa, ngazi au kwa wote (maalum zaidi hushinda; mstari unaweza kuwa kila muhula au muhula mmoja). Toa **ufadhili/punguzo** kwa kila mwanafunzi na mwaka (asilimia au kiasi, kwa sababu).
**Kila muhula** — *Ankara → Tengeneza ankara za muhula*: chagua muhula na tarehe ya mwisho, soma **hakiki** (nani atatozwa, nani hana muundo, ankara za jumla sifuri), kisha tengeneza. Kuendesha tena hakumtozi mtu mara mbili. Tumia *Marekebisho* (mistari ya ziada) na *Awamu* inapohitajika; *Batilisha* ankara kwa sababu.
**Kupokea pesa** — *Malipo → Rekodi malipo*: tafuta mwanafunzi, weka kiasi, njia (taslimu, benki, pesa za simu) na namba ya hati ya benki au muamala. **Risiti** yenye namba (PDF, Kiingereza au Kiswahili) hutengenezwa. Hati au namba ileile haiwezi kuingizwa mara mbili. Pesa isiyoelekezwa kwa ankara hulipa ankara ya zamani zaidi ambayo haijalipwa; ziada hubaki kama **salio**.
**Makosa** — usihariri kamwe: **Tengua** malipo kwa sababu (haiwezekani baada ya pesa yake kurejeshwa). **Marejesho** hurudisha salio tu: ombi → mtu *mwingine* anaidhinisha → alama imelipwa.
**Arifa za pesa za simu/benki** — hadi mtoa huduma aunganishwe hakuna kinachofika kiotomatiki. Akiunganishwa, kisichoeleweka kinasubiri kwenye **Upatanisho** ukilinganishe au ukikatae; hakuna kinachohesabiwa kama kimelipwa hadi kilinganishwe.
**Matumizi** — rekodi kwa kundi; hayawezi kufutwa.
**Ripoti** — makusanyo kwa siku na njia, kwa darasa, madeni (kwa umri), matumizi. Kila moja inaweza kupakuliwa kama CSV, Excel au PDF; upakuaji unarekodiwa.
**Vikumbusho** — *Mawasiliano → Vikumbusho vya ada*: chagua wanaodaiwa (waliochelewa au wote), hakiki wazazi na kiasi cha kila mtoto, tuma au panga. SMS huonyesha *imerukwa* hadi mtoa huduma wa SMS aunganishwe.

Kumbuka: *imelipwa*, *imelipwa sehemu*, *haijalipwa* na *hakuna malipo* ni hali tofauti. Ankara kuwepo au kuwa sifuri hakumaanishi malipo yamepokelewa.
