The system at a glance

One system for the whole school, with one job for each department.

The St Anne Marie Schools system brings the school's daily work into one place: applications and admissions, student records, attendance, exams and results, report cards, fees and money, messages to parents, the timetable, homework and lesson plans, the library, the public website and a portal for parents and students. It works in a web browser on a computer, a tablet or a phone, in English and Kiswahili.

What problem does it solve?

Before

Paper forms and registers. The same name typed again in every office. Marks copied by hand. Nobody sure who has paid. Parents phone to ask for news. The owner learns about problems late.

With the system

A student is entered once and every department sees the same record. Marks, fees and attendance are calculated by the system. Parents see their own child's information. The owner sees the whole school on one screen.

The rules the system keeps

These rules are built into the system itself. They protect the school's records and keep every department accountable.

1 · One job each

Every department does only its own work. Nobody can do another department's job just because they can see the student.

2 · One record

A student has one record and one Student ID for all years. Nothing is typed twice and nothing is copied.

3 · Hand-offs, not phone calls

When one department finishes its part, the next one receives a task with a due date.

4 · Nothing is erased

Money, receipts, results after approval and behaviour records are never edited. They are corrected by a reversal or a revision, so the history stays.

5 · Two people for money

The person who records an expense or a refund can never be the person who approves it.

6 · Everything is logged

Who did what, to which record, and when, is written to the audit log and cannot be changed.

7 · You see what your job needs

Each person's access is set by their department and enforced by the system, not only hidden on the screen.

8 · Nothing is invented

If there is no data, the screen shows a dash or an empty list. It never shows made-up figures.

9 · The owner watches

The owner sees everything but does not do the daily work. In an emergency he can step in on purpose, with a reason, and it is recorded.

Getting started

Signing in, finding your way, and using the system on a phone or tablet.

2.1 Opening the system

Open a web browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge) on any computer, tablet or phone and go to the school's system address: https://st-anne-marie-os.vercel.app. The first screen is the school's public website. To work in the system, press Log in or go straight to /login.

2.2 Signing in

  1. Press Log in →. A small window opens over the picture.
  2. Type your email or phone number, then your password. Use Show to check what you typed.
  3. Press Sign in. The system takes you to your home page: every person lands on a different page according to their job.
  4. Wrong password? The window stays open so you can try again. After several wrong tries the system makes you wait. If you forgot it, ask the school office to set a new one for you.
Change your password

Use the account menu (your name at the bottom of the side menu, or the round button at the top on a phone) and choose Change password. Choose something only you know. Never share your password and always press Sign out on a shared computer.

2.3 The parts of the screen

PartWhat it does
Side menu (left)Your pages, grouped: Students, Academic, Exams, Learning, Finance, Communication. It shows only the pages your department may use. The small arrow at the top collapses it to icons. Your name, language (EN | SW), light/dark switch and Sign out are at the bottom.
Top barSearch students by name or number, the school year and term, a link to the public website, and the bell for notifications. On a phone the side menu opens from the button at the top-left.

2.4 On a phone or tablet

The system is built for phones first. Cards sit two across, alerts become one line each, tables scroll sideways inside their box, and buttons are large enough for a thumb. The same login works everywhere.

2.5 Handy things everywhere

  • Search students at the top: type a name or Student ID.
  • Notifications (the bell): messages and tasks for you.
  • Download buttons: any list that has Excel, PDF or CSV can be saved. The downloaded list is exactly what you are allowed to see, nothing more.
  • Light and dark: the sun or moon button switches the look. Dark is easier on the eyes in a dim office.
  • Kiswahili: press SW next to EN. The whole screen changes language.

4
Admissions Academic

5
Parent
Sees only published report cards and downloads the PDF.

Academic
Looks at each student's results and decides: moves up, repeats, finishes school, withdraws. The system never decides this by itself. The Head Teacher approves too if the school asks.
2

3
Parent

5
Admissions
Confirms the registration and, when the year starts, joins the student to the class (the class must have room).
6
Teachers
See the new class lists.

Important

For fees, Admissions sees only whether a returning student is cleared, and the Academic Office sees only a "fees need review" flag. Neither sees amounts. For marks, Finance sees none. This is enforced by the system, not just hidden on the screen.

3.4 Tasks: how a hand-off is tracked

Each hand-off is a task: it has a department, a person (if assigned), a due date, a status (waiting, in progress, done) and a history. A task is closed only when the real step is done (for example, the payment is verified), never just because someone opened a page. The tasks of every department are listed on the Tasks page. Overdue tasks appear on the owner's home with the department and the person holding them.

3.5 Approvals and limits

Not everything needs the owner. Routine decisions stay inside the department. The owner decides only what is reserved for him, and he sets the limit himself:

  • Expenses and refunds below the limit: the Head Teacher approves (never the person who recorded them).
  • Expenses and refunds at or above the limit: only the Owner can decide.
  • Exceptional admission overrides, fee discounts and school-wide settings: Owner level.
