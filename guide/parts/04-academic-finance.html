Academic Office

The school year, the timetable, exams and results, report cards and promotion.

Your job in one line

Build the academic year and timetable, run exams, approve and publish results, decide who moves up, and keep the school's academic records right.

6.1 What you see when you sign in

6.2 Your routine

  • Start of the year: set up the year, terms, classes, streams, subjects and who teaches what; build the timetable.
  • Each term: create the exams; follow the mark sheets as teachers submit; approve, lock and publish.
  • End of the year: prepare and decide promotion for every student.
  • All year: transfers, concerns raised by teachers, recruitment recommendations, and entrance exams for applicants.

6.3 Step by step

Set up the academic year

  1. Open Academics (academic set-up).
  2. Add the academic year and its terms with start and end dates. Mark the current term.
  3. Add the levels (Nursery, Primary, O-Level, A-Level), then the classes and their streams, with the capacity of each. Choose which grade follows each class (this drives promotion).
  4. Add the subjects and assign teachers to classes and subjects. A class teacher covers every subject of the class; a subject teacher only their own.
  5. Open the readiness checklist: it lists what is still missing before the year can run.

Build the timetable

  1. Define the periods (start and end times, breaks).
  2. Place each lesson: class, subject, teacher, room, day and period. The system refuses a clash: the same teacher or room in two places, or two lessons for one class at one time.
  3. Teachers see their own week on their home page. Use Print for a class or teacher view.

Create an exam and follow the marks

  1. Open Examinations and create the exam: name, term, the subjects it covers and the maximum mark. Choose the grading scale (or let it use the level's or the school's default).
  2. The system creates one mark sheet for each class and stream. Assign the teacher for each (the class and subject teacher is filled in from the assignments).
  3. Add the exam timetable (date, time, venue). Families see their child's class sittings.

Approve, lock and read the results

  1. Open Marks. A sheet shows Submitted when the teacher has finished.
  2. Open the sheet and check it. Approve it, or Return it to the teacher with a note.
  3. When a unit is approved and final, Lock it. Official results count only locked sheets.
  4. After locking, any correction needs a reason (only the Head Teacher can unlock) and every change is kept in the history.

The Grading page holds the grading scales and divisions used for report cards.

Publish report cards

  1. When every subject of a class is locked, press Generate. The card is built from the official results, attendance and the teachers' remarks.
  2. Class teachers add their remarks (and the Head Teacher adds the head's comment).
  3. Press Publish. From then on parents see the card and can download the PDF. You can withdraw a card if a mistake is found.
Fees and report cards

If the school switches on "require fees cleared", a card is published only for students whose fees are within the limit. The school decides this rule; it is shown, never hidden.

Decide who moves up (end of the year)

  1. Open Year-end decisions. The system lists every student and whether their results are complete.
  2. For each student choose moves up, repeats, finishes school, withdraws or transferred. The system never decides this for you. If the school sets a promotion average, only students who reach it are pre-filled, and the rest wait for your decision.
  3. Anything unusual (missing results, a grade outside the order, promoting below the average) is an exception and needs the exception permission and a written reason, which is kept.
  4. Approve. If the school asks for it, the Head Teacher approves too. Approving applies the decision: Admissions receives the next-year registration.

Transfers, concerns, recruitment and entrance exams

6.4 What you cannot do, and why

  • Verify payments or approve expenses. That is Finance and the Head Teacher. You see a "fees need review" flag at most, never an amount.
  • Process applications. Admissions does. You assign teachers to entrance exams and review their marks.

You receive

  • Marks from teachers.
  • Applicants to assess from Admissions.
  • Concerns from teachers.

You give

  • Classes, subjects, timetable and exams to teachers.
  • Approved promotion decisions to Admissions.
  • Published report cards to parents.

Finance Office

Fees, invoices, payments, receipts, expenses, refunds and reports.

Your job in one line

Set the fees, bill the students, record and verify every payment, issue receipts, record expenses, and keep the school's money records exact.

7.1 What you see when you sign in

7.2 Your routine

  • Every day: record the payments received; match any mobile-money payment that could not be matched.
  • Each term: generate the term invoices; send fee reminders (the system also runs a daily check).
  • Each week: read the arrears and collections reports; chase the largest balances.
  • Each month: record the expenses; ask for refunds only from money a family has overpaid.
One balance, everywhere

All balances come from one calculation, so the number is the same on the invoice, the parent's screen, the report and the owner's home. Payments are applied to a chosen invoice first, then to the oldest invoice. The system never marks something paid because an invoice exists or its amount is zero.

7.3 Step by step

Set up fees (once a year)

  1. Open Finance → Fees. Add the fee items (tuition, transport, uniform…).
  2. Build the fee structure for each class and term: which items, how much, and the instalment dates.
  3. In the settings, enter the due days, the share that must be paid before a returning student is cleared, the amount at which an expense needs approval, and the How to pay text exactly as the bank or mobile-money account should be shown. Bank numbers are never built into the system; you type them.

Generate and manage invoices

  1. In Generate term invoices choose the class (or all classes) and the due date, press Preview to see who will be billed, then confirm. There is one live invoice for each student for each term, so doing it twice never bills a student twice.
  2. Open an invoice to see the lines, instalments, payments and history. Invoice lines are never edited: to correct a bill you adjust (a new line with a reason) or void it (with a reason).
  3. Print the invoice or the fee table for the class.

Record a payment and issue a receipt

  1. Open Payments and press Record a payment.
  2. Choose the student, the amount, the method (cash, bank or mobile money) and the date. For bank or mobile money, type the reference.
  3. Optionally choose the invoice the money is for; otherwise it clears the oldest bill first.
  4. Press Save. A numbered receipt is created (A5, in the school's format, with the amount in words and the balance before and after).
  5. A wrong payment is never edited: reverse it with a reason and record it again.

Reconcile mobile-money payments

When a payment provider is connected, its notifications are posted automatically when they match exactly one student. Any that cannot be matched wait in Reconciliation: open the item, choose the student it belongs to, or reject it with a note. Nothing is ever marked paid on a family's word, only on the provider's signed confirmation.

Expenses and refunds

  1. Expense: choose a category, enter the amount, payee, date and method. A category can be marked "needs approval", and the school sets an amount above which approval is always needed. The Head Teacher approves (or the Owner, above his limit). You cannot approve your own.
  2. Refund: only money a student has paid beyond what is owed can be refunded. Request it with a reason; after approval record the payout with its reference.

Reports and next-year clearance

  • Cleared means enough of the first-term bill is paid (the school's rule). If a family agrees a payment plan you can record an arrangement (with a reason) that treats the condition as met, without changing any balance.
  • Application fees confirmed by the provider are listed (read-only) under Finance → Application fees.

7.4 What you cannot do, and why

  • Accept applicants, enter marks or change a student's record. You see the student's name, class and fees only.
  • Approve your own expense or refund. A second person always decides.
  • Edit or delete a payment, receipt or invoice line. You reverse, void or adjust, so the history stays complete.

You receive

  • Fee tasks from Admissions.
  • Approved expenses and refunds from the Head Teacher or Owner.
  • Provider payment notifications.

You give

  • The Owner: money figures and reports.
  • Admissions: who is cleared to register.
  • Parents: what they owe and receipts.
