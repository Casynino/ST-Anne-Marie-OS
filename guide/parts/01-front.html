About this guide

This guide has two jobs. It is a presentation of what the school now has, written so the owner and the project manager can see the whole picture. It is also a manual: every department can open its chapter and learn, step by step, how to do its work in the system.

For the owner and the project manager

Chapters 1, 3, 4, 12 and 13 show how the school is controlled, who is accountable, and what to set up first.

For each department

Chapters 5 to 10 are one chapter per department: the daily routine, the steps with screenshots, what you cannot do, and who you hand over to.

For everyone

Chapter 2 explains the screen on a computer, a tablet and a phone. Chapter 14 answers common questions.

How to read the colours

Each department has its own colour in this guide so you can follow work as it moves: Owner Admissions Academic Office Finance Head Teacher Teachers Parents & students The system

About the screenshots

The screenshots come from a demonstration school with made-up pupils, parents and money, loaded only to show how the screens look when they are in use. The live system starts empty: no student, fee or payment is invented.
