The Owner

Overseeing the whole school without doing the departments' daily work.

Your job in one line

Watch how the school is doing, decide what is reserved for you, keep accounts and settings in order, and make sure every department is doing its own work.

4.1 What you see when you sign in

  • Alert lines show only what is yours: expenses or refunds above your limit, hiring decisions, payments Finance has not matched, reversals, a department with overdue work (with the oldest task and who holds it), and a missing backup.
  • Income strip: money received in the chosen period, split by cash, bank and mobile money.
  • Press This term, This month or This year to change the period for the whole money section.

4.2 Your routine

  • Each morning (2 minutes): read the alert lines. If a department shows overdue work, press the line to see the tasks and who holds them. Ask that person, not the whole department.
  • Each week: look at the Departments table and the Money section for the week.
  • When asked: decide expenses or refunds above your limit, and staff-hiring decisions.
  • Each term: read the audit log and check that a recent backup exists.

4.3 Step by step

Create a staff account

  1. Open Staff and users and press New user.
  2. Type the full name and an email address or phone number.
  3. Choose the department (Head Teacher, Admissions, Academic Office, Finance or Teacher). The department decides exactly what the person can do.
  4. Type a password (at least 10 characters with letters and numbers), or leave it empty to get a random one shown once.
  5. Give the person their login privately and ask them to change the password.

To reset a forgotten password or suspend someone who has left, open the person from the Staff and users list. Roles and permissions shows exactly what each department can do.

Set your approval limit

  1. Open Settings → Approval limits.
  2. Type the amount for expenses and for refunds. At or above it, only you decide. Below it, the Head Teacher decides. 0 means no limit.
  3. Press Save. Decisions already made do not change.

Decide an expense or refund above your limit

Press the alert line "expense(s) above your limit". Open the item, read who recorded it and why, and approve or reject. You cannot approve something you recorded yourself, and you never see routine items that the Head Teacher decides.

Read the audit log

Step in during an emergency

  1. Open Step in… at the bottom of your side menu.
  2. Write a short, honest reason (for example "the Admissions Office is away and a family is waiting").
  3. Press Step in for 30 minutes. A banner shows on every page. Do only the one urgent job.
  4. Press Stop stepping in as soon as you finish. The start and the end are written to the audit log.

4.4 What you cannot do, and why

By design

You cannot place or register students, record or verify payments, enter marks, take attendance or publish report cards. Those jobs belong to the departments, which keeps each one accountable and keeps you free to oversee. If a department is truly stuck, use Step in.

You receive

  • Alerts and figures from every department.
  • Items above your limit.
  • Hiring decisions.

You give

  • Accounts, roles and passwords.
  • The approval limit.
  • The school's settings and website text.
5

Admissions Office

From the first enquiry to a registered student, and the return of every student each year.

Your job in one line

Receive and review applications, arrange interviews, accept or decline, register students, register returning students each year, and keep student and parent records correct.

5.1 What you see when you sign in

5.2 Your routine

  • Every morning: open the alerts. Review new applications, chase interviews that have passed without a result, and register students who passed.
  • During registration season: work the Returning to-do list.
  • Any time: answer enquiries, update parent contacts, add events to the school calendar.

5.3 Step by step

Review a new application

  1. Open Admissions. Every new application, online or typed at the office, is in the Applications tab. Two statuses are used: Under review and Sent back.
  2. Press View to see everything: the applicant's details, the guardian, the papers, the history.
  3. Check the required papers. The bar shows how complete the file is and names what is missing.
  4. Choose one action: Accept, Send back (the family is told what to add) or Decline (a reason is required).
Accept is greyed out?

An application can be accepted only when it is complete. Hover over the button to see what is missing. If it is a paper, Send back to the family, or add the paper yourself on the application page.

Accept and set the interview date

  1. Press Accept on the row.
  2. Tick "I have checked the papers". If an interview date came with the application it is kept; otherwise choose a date (an open-day date or any other date).
  3. Confirm. The application moves to the Interviews tab. If the school does not require an interview for that class, the applicant is accepted and registered at once.

Record an interview result

  1. After the interview, press Result (it appears once the interview time has passed).
  2. If an entrance exam is required, all subject results must be in first. Open the interview page to see which teacher still owes marks.
  3. Record pass or not accepted with a note. A pass accepts and registers the student at once.

Register the student (Student ID and class together)

  1. Open the Registration tab.
  2. Check the class. The class asked for is pre-selected; a full class is refused (only the Owner can override, with a reason).
  3. Press Register. The Student ID is created and the student has a class in one step.
  4. Several at once: tick the applicants, choose one class, and register them together. The system refuses the whole request if the class does not have enough places, and tells you which classes do.

Type an application at the office

  1. Press New application.
  2. Fill the same form families fill online, including the photo and papers.
  3. The form is saved as a draft with a reference and a printout. The family pays the application fee at Finance (if the school sets a fee).
  4. When Finance records the fee, the draft becomes an application on your list.

Returning students: the next-year to-do list

  1. After the Academic Office approves a student's move or repeat, the student appears here.
  2. Place: confirm the grade and stream. The decided grade is pre-selected; a different grade needs an exception with a reason.
  3. Register: confirm the registration with the family. (Parents can also register from their own page.)
  4. Join class: when the new year starts, join the student to the class (only when there is room).
  5. For families who have not registered, use Follow up and record the outcome ("Will register", "Not returning", "No answer"). A note never cancels anyone: cancelling is a separate step with a reason.

Students, parents, enquiries and the calendar

  • Import existing students: Students → Import takes a CSV file. Nothing is imported until you have seen a preview with errors and duplicates, and then it is imported in one go, once.
  • Parent forgot the password: open the parent from the Parents list and press Reset password.

5.4 What you cannot do, and why

  • Verify or record payments. That is Finance. You can see whether a returning student is cleared, not the amount.
  • Enter marks or decide promotion. That is the Academic Office; you receive the decision.
  • Record teachers' attendance. That is the Head Teacher.

You receive

  • Applications from families (online) and the application-fee confirmation from Finance.
  • Entrance marks from teachers via the Academic Office.
  • Approved promotion and repeat decisions.

You give

  • Registered students with Student ID and class to every department.
  • Class lists to teachers.
  • Applicants to assess to the Academic Office.
