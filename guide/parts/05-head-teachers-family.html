8

Head Teacher

Oversight of the teachers' work and the second approval on marks, plans and money.

Your job in one line

Make sure teachers do their part on time, approve the work that needs a second person, and keep the school's academic standard.

8.1 What you see when you sign in

8.2 Your routine

  • Every morning: check which registers are not finished and which teachers have not signed in.
  • Daily: approve expenses waiting for you (below the Owner's limit).
  • Weekly: review lesson plans and schemes of work; follow the mark sheets.
  • Each term: approve marks with the Academic Office, add the head's comment on report cards, publish when ready.

8.3 Step by step

Approve an expense or a refund

  1. Open the alert "expense(s) waiting for your approval", or Finance → Expenses.
  2. Open the item: who recorded it, the payee, the amount and the reason.
  3. Press Approve, or Reject with a reason. It only counts in the totals once approved.
  4. Items at or above the Owner's limit are not shown to you: they go to the Owner. You can never decide something you recorded.

Approve marks and unlock a locked result

Review lesson plans and schemes of work

Correct a finished register, and record teachers' attendance

  1. Open Attendance. Every register is listed with its status for the day.
  2. Open a finished register and press Correct. Choose the new status and give a reason. The correction is added to a history that cannot be edited.
  3. Under Attendance → Staff, record whether each teacher was present, absent or excused.

Other things you do

  • Second approval of year-end decisions (if the school asks for it) and the head's comment on report cards.
  • Hiring decisions after the Academic Office recommends (never for a candidate you recorded).
  • Messages: you can read and moderate conversations between teachers and parents when needed.

8.4 What you cannot do, and why

  • Record, verify or reverse payments, or set fees. That is Finance. You approve expenses and refunds; you do not make them.
  • Hold the Owner's powers (above-limit approvals, accounts and settings).
9

Teachers

Attendance, marks, homework, lesson plans and behaviour for your own classes.

Your job in one line

Take attendance, teach and assess your own classes, enter marks on time, and keep parents informed.

9.1 What you see when you sign in

You see only your own classes and students. A class teacher covers every subject of their class; a subject teacher covers only their own subject.

9.2 Your routine

  • Start of the day: take and finish your class register.
  • During the term: set homework, mark hand-ins, update your lesson plans.
  • Exam time: enter marks and press Submit before the date.
  • Any time: record behaviour, flag a concern, reply to parents.

9.3 Step by step

Take attendance

  1. Open Attendance and choose your class.
  2. Mark each student: Present, Absent, Late or Excused. Every student must be marked before you can finish.
  3. Press Finalise. Parents of absent children are told in their account.
  4. No internet? Before you go offline, download the class lists for the day (the Attendance screen has a button for it) while you still have internet. Take attendance as normal; it is kept on your device and sent when the internet returns. It only shows "synced" when the school's server has confirmed it.
  5. A mistake after finishing is corrected by the Head Teacher (with a reason), not edited by you.

Enter marks

  1. Open Marks and choose the sheet for your class and subject.
  2. Type each student's mark in the grid. A missing mark is not a zero. If a student was absent or exempt, choose that, so the report is true.
  3. Press Save: it is saved as one whole (all or nothing). You can come back and change it while it is a draft.
  4. Press Submit when done. After you submit you cannot change it unless the Academic Office or Head Teacher returns it to you.
If the sheet says "returned"

Open it, read the note, correct the marks and submit again. Every change after the first submission is kept in a history.

Set and mark homework

  1. Press New, choose the class and subject, write the task, set the due date, then Publish. Students see it in their account.
  2. Students hand in through their own account (text; photos or files when the school has switched on file storage).
  3. Mark each hand-in. Marks stay private until you release them; parents see the mark only after release.

Lesson plans, behaviour and messages

  • Behaviour: record positive notes and concerns. A record cannot be edited or deleted; a mistake is voided with a reason. Students see only positive notes that are shared.
  • Concerns: if you worry about a student (absence, learning, welfare), flag it. The Academic Office follows up; the family sees only a message the office chooses to share.
  • Messages: you can write to the parents of students you teach. Messages are rate-limited and can be moderated by the Head Teacher.
  • Entrance exams: if you are assigned a subject for an applicant, mark it under Applicant exams after the interview time. Your marks are private until you submit.

9.4 What you cannot do, and why

  • See other teachers' classes, school-wide fees or money records.
  • Change a student's registration, class or admission decision, or promote a student.
  • Edit results after they are approved and locked.
10

Parents and students

The family portal: what families see and what they can do.

The idea in one line

Every family has one login on their phone. A parent sees their own children; a student sees their own page. They see only information the school has released.

10.1 What families see

WhereWhat it shows
HomeThe parent's children; for a student, their own page.
Your childAttendance, latest report, homework, fees, timetable, behaviour, portfolio and the exam timetable.
FeesThe bill, what has been paid, what is still due, receipts, and the school's own "How to pay" text. Money counts only after Finance has verified it.
Report cardsOnly published cards, with a PDF download and a progress report.
InboxAnnouncements, absence alerts and reminders from the school.
MessagesConversations with teachers and the office.
School calendarTerm dates, events and the exam timetable.
SettingsNotification choices, language and password.

10.2 Registering a child for next year

  1. After the Academic Office approves the move (or repeat), the child's page shows a card "Next year" with four steps: school decision, registration, first payment, class and stream.
  2. While registration is open, the parent ticks "we confirm that the child is returning and the details are correct" and presses register. The child is placed in the decided grade in the stream with the most room.
  3. The first payment is made as usual; it counts when Finance has verified it.
  4. When the new year starts the office joins the child to the class. The family sees "in the class".
When it is refused

Registration is refused outside the dates the school sets, when the class is full (the family is told to contact the office), or when the school has chosen to require a payment first. Only the child's own family can register the child.

10.3 Students

  • Students sign in with their own account and hand in homework from the child page. Parents can see the same information but cannot hand in work for the student.
  • Students see homework, timetable, published results and positive behaviour notes that the school shares. They do not see fee amounts of other students or any other child's information.

10.4 Applying online and the family's account

A family that applies online is given an account in the same step. Under Applications they see the status, can edit details and papers before confirming, pay the application fee (when the school sets one) by quoting the payment reference, download the application form and receipt, and read the school's messages. Chapter 11 shows the screens.

Forgot the password?

Ask the school office. Admissions (or the Owner) opens the parent in the Parents list and sets a new password. Nobody can read an existing password.
