11

The public website and online application

What visitors and applicants see, and how it connects to Admissions.

The school's public website is part of the same system. It uses only the school's own text and photographs. Where the school has not yet entered something, the section stays hidden instead of showing invented content. Visitors can switch to a calm dark look with the moon button; light is the default.

11.1 How a family applies

  1. The family opens Apply online and sees the windows that are open now. They can also apply at any time: with no open window, Admissions gives the interview date later.
  2. They fill the form: the child's details, guardians (an email address is needed because the account is created in the same step), the previous school, health note, emergency contact and the papers. A transfer student also gives the previous school, last class and reason, and adds the transfer letter and previous report.
  3. They confirm on a review page. If the school sets an application fee, they pay by quoting the reference; the application reaches Admissions only after the payment provider confirms the money.
  4. They can track the application at any time from their account, or with the application number and phone on the Track page.
Who edits the website?

The Owner (and the Head Teacher) edit the text and photographs under Website; calendar events are added by Admissions, the Academic Office or the Head Teacher. Images must be the school's own; photographs of children are used only with the school's consent.

12

Security, privacy and accountability

How the school's data, money and children's information are protected.

Sign-in

Passwords are stored scrambled (never readable). Sessions are protected cookies. Repeated wrong passwords make the account wait. The connection is encrypted (https).

Permissions

Every page and every action checks the person's department on the school's server. A hidden button is never the only protection; a person cannot open a page by guessing its address.

Audit log

Every important action (who, what, which record, when, and the reason where one is asked) is recorded and cannot be changed or erased.

Nothing is rewritten

Money, receipts, approved results and behaviour records are corrected by a reversal or revision, so the history is always complete.

Two people for money

Expenses and refunds are approved by a second person. The Owner decides above his own limit.

Children's privacy

Medical notes are visible only to Admissions and the Head Teacher, and each view is logged. Parents see only their own children. Downloaded files are checked for permission each time.

The owner's step-in

The Owner can enter a department's work only for 30 minutes, with a written reason, a banner on every page, and a record at the start and the end.

Backups

The database provider keeps a short history for recovery; a separate backup copy should also be made regularly (see chapter 13). The Owner's home warns if the newest backup is too old.

Honest messages

The system never says an SMS was delivered or a fee was paid unless the provider's signed confirmation says so.

13

Setting up the school and going live

A practical checklist, in the order it should be done.

The live system starts empty and honest: one school, the departments' accounts, and no students, fees or payments. Real information is entered by the department that owns it. The order below avoids waiting: each step unlocks the next.

1
Owner
Day 1. Sign in, change the password, check the school profile (name, motto, contact, currency, timezone). Give each department its login privately and ask them to change the password.
2
Academic
Week 1. Academic year and terms; levels, classes and streams with capacity; subjects; grading scale; teacher assignments. Open the readiness checklist until it is complete. Then the periods and the timetable.
3
Finance
Week 1. Fee items; the fee structure for each class and term; the "How to pay" text exactly as the bank or mobile-money account; the due days, the first-payment share for returning students, and the expense approval amount.
4
Owner
Week 1. Create each teacher's account (Staff and users, department Teacher). Set the approval limit. Enter the website text and photographs.
5
Admissions
Week 2. Admissions policy (required papers, interview or exam by level, application fee) and the application windows. Import the existing students and parents from a CSV file; check the preview, then confirm. Check the counts against the paper registers.
6
Head
Week 2. Check each class has a class teacher and the timetable is complete. Review the teachers' accounts and who has not signed in.
7
Everyone
Week 2. A 1-hour session per department using its chapter in this guide. Each person signs in on their own phone or computer and does one real task.
8
Teachers Finance
Week 3. Go live with attendance each morning and with payments each day. Admissions creates parent accounts or families register through applications.
9
Owner
Week 4. Read the first full report: the Money, School and Departments sections. Fix what is behind. Check that a backup exists.

Quick reference

Common questions, key words, and a one-page cheat sheet for each department.

14.1 Questions and answers

QuestionAnswer
I forgot my password.Ask the school office. The Owner resets staff passwords; Admissions or the Owner resets a parent's.
I cannot see a page my colleague can.Your department does not include it. That is on purpose. Ask the Owner if your department's access should change.
A teacher entered a wrong mark and it is already approved.Ask the Head Teacher to unlock the unit with a reason. The change is recorded.
A payment was recorded for the wrong student.Finance reverses it with a reason and records it again. It is never edited.
A parent cannot see the report card.Only published cards are visible. Check with the Academic Office; if fees must be cleared first, Finance and the Owner decide that rule.
Accept is greyed out on an application.The file is incomplete. Hover to see what is missing; send it back or add the paper.
The class is full.Only the Owner can override, with a reason. Otherwise choose another stream or class.
A student arrives in the middle of the year.Admissions registers the student; the student joins the class list and can be added to a finished register later.
A teacher has left.The Owner suspends the account. Marks and history stay. The Academic Office reassigns the classes.
There is no internet.Teachers can take attendance offline after downloading the class lists. It syncs when the internet returns.
Where is the proof of who did something?The audit log (Owner and Head Teacher).
Can the Owner fix a department's work?Not as normal work. In an emergency he can step in for 30 minutes with a reason; it is recorded.

The permanent record of who did what.

14.3 Department cheat sheets

Owner: your day

1) Read the alert lines. 2) Press a department line to see who holds overdue work. 3) Decide items above your limit. 4) Weekly: Money and Departments sections. 5) Termly: audit log and backups. 6) Step in only in an emergency.

Admissions: your day

1) New applications: view, then accept, send back or decline. 2) Interviews with no result: record. 3) Passed: register with a class. 4) Returning list: Place, Register, Join class. 5) Answer enquiries. 6) Keep parent phones correct.

Academic Office: your day

1) Set up year, classes, subjects, teachers, timetable. 2) Create exams. 3) Approve and lock mark sheets. 4) Generate and publish report cards. 5) Year-end decisions. 6) Transfers, concerns, entrance exams.

Finance: your day

1) Record payments; issue receipts. 2) Match mobile-money payments. 3) Generate term invoices. 4) Record expenses; request refunds. 5) Read arrears and collections. 6) Tell Admissions who is cleared.

Head Teacher: your day

1) See which registers are open. 2) Approve expenses below the limit. 3) Approve marks and plans. 4) Chase teachers using the teachers' table. 5) Correct a register with a reason. 6) Add the head's comment and publish.

Teacher: your day

1) Take and finalise the register. 2) Teach; set homework. 3) Enter marks; save; submit. 4) Mark hand-ins; release marks. 5) Update plans. 6) Record behaviour; flag concerns; reply to parents.

Parent: your day

1) Check attendance and homework. 2) See fees and how to pay. 3) Read the inbox. 4) Message the teacher. 5) At year-end register the child. 6) Download the report card when published.
